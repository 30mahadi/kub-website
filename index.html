ABOUT THE UNIVERSITY

Building Tomorrow's Leaders

KING'S UNIVERSITY, BANGLADESH is a proposed non-profit private university established in 2025 under a charitable trust. The institution operates with a secured Reserve Fund deposited at IFIC Bank Limited in compliance with national regulatory requirements.


The university is committed to developing future leaders through quality education, research, and innovation. Academic activities will commence at a 25,000 square feet temporary campus in Dhaka before transitioning to a permanent campus.

Mission

To provide accessible, research-driven, and globally competitive education rooted in integrity and excellence.

Vision

To become a leading private university in Bangladesh recognized for academic leadership, innovation, and responsible citizenship.
